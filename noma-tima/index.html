Twenty-two years on desserts

Twenty-two years is a lot of custard. Long enough to know when caramel has gone one shade too far, how long a sponge should sit in three milks, and why a celebration cake tastes better layered the day you order it. Chef Nesar Ahmad Noorullah spent those years in five-star hotel kitchens, luxury bakeries and premium online bakeries. NOMA TIMA is his own kitchen: tres leches and Matilda pies, molten cheesecakes, kunafa under dark chocolate, and a tiramisu called TimaMisu.

The details are specific. Strawberry compote from Mahabaleshwar strawberries. Coorg coffee in the cup. RO water in the bread dough, and homemade buns with no chemicals. Many of the desserts are eggless. The brûlée gets its sugar crust under the torch, to order, so the shell is still crackling when your spoon reaches it.

He also runs TBC, The Bakery Consultant, setting up kitchens for other bakeries. His Instagram bio lists his current obsession as the perfect dessert. This menu is where he works on it.
